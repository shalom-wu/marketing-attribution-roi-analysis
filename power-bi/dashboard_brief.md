# Dashboard Brief - Marketing Attribution

## Audience and purpose

This dashboard is for a marketing analytics or growth strategy reviewer deciding how to discuss channel credit and budget reallocation.

## Pages

### 1 - Executive KPI

Show journeys, channels, conversion rate when seen, current budget, and Markov-removal budget gap.

### 2 - Attribution Diagnostics

Compare first-touch, last-touch, linear, and Markov-removal credit by channel. Include journey length and common path patterns.

### 3 - Budget Decision Support

Show conservative, balanced, and aggressive reallocation scenarios, budget shifted, estimated lift, and incremental ROAS.

## Honesty notes

Budget and revenue values are assumptions layered onto Criteo ad-event data. Treat recommendations as a budget-testing framework, not causal proof.
